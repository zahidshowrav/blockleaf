Subscript and superscript, from the new `@blockleaf/editor/text-style` subpath. `withTextStyle(extensions)` adds both marks, with `Mod-,` and `Mod-.` and two bubble-menu buttons that appear only where the marks are loaded. They exclude each other, so a run is never both. New in schema version 2; adds 0.20 kB on top of the editor.
